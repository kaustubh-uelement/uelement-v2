The shared spine

What “one control plane” actually buys you.

Not a marketing phrase. Four concrete things you only have to decide, build, and audit once.

Identity

One tenant, one directory

A single tenant model, RBAC and ABAC, and FIDO2 passwordless access across all three fabrics. Adopt one product and the second inherits your identity architecture.

Evidence

One audit trail

Compliance evidence from every fabric lands in the same tamper-evident ledger. One artifact satisfies digital, operational, and physical-custody obligations at once.

Deployment

One topology decision

All three deploy air-gapped, on-premise, on sovereign cloud, hybrid-managed, or as hosted SaaS — on a single control plane, with no re-platforming between them.

Interfaces

One API surface

REST, GraphQL, and webhooks with shared conventions. A Ankura workflow can open a Vizor incident and trigger a Kayak fulfillment without custom integration.

Built for regulated ground

Sovereign-ready is the default, not the enterprise tier.

Every MainSTAY product runs inside an air-gapped enclave, on customer-owned infrastructure, in a sovereign-aligned data centre, or as hosted SaaS — using the same control plane and the same APIs. Moving between those models is a deployment decision, not a migration project.

Compliance packs for IEC 62443, CERT-In, the DPDP Act, RBI and SEBI frameworks, NIST, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 ship with the platform and map to live telemetry. Post-quantum cryptography posture is inherited from UElement’s U92 practice rather than bolted on later.
