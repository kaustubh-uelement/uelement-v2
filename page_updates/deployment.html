Deployment

Five topologies. One control plane.

Every MainSTAY product ships in the same five shapes, sharing one control plane and one API surface. Moving between them is a deployment decision, not a migration project.

The topologies

Pick the shape your regulator, not your vendor, requires.

Defence · Nuclear · Sovereign

On-Prem Air-Gapped

Runs entirely offline inside the customer’s secure enclave. Updates, threat-library refreshes, and model weights arrive via controlled physical media on a governed cadence.

  • No outbound network dependency of any kind
  • Evidence Channel Bridge carries compliance artifacts across data diodes
  • OpenLM keeps every AI capability inside the perimeter

Tier-1 BFSI · Government

On-Prem Connected

Customer-owned infrastructure with controlled, allow-listed egress for updates and threat intelligence. The customer retains physical custody of every plane.

  • Allow-listed egress only, with full audit of outbound calls
  • Customer-managed keys and certificate lifecycle
  • Suits RBI, SEBI, and MeitY infrastructure expectations

Regulated enterprise

Sovereign Cloud

Deployed inside sovereign-aligned data centres — CtrlS, Yotta, AWS Asia Pacific, or Azure India — where data residency is contractual and verifiable.

  • In-country residency for data, evidence, and backups
  • Regional failover without leaving the jurisdiction
  • Shortest path to production for most Indian enterprises

Regulated enterprise

Hybrid Managed

The customer retains the data and evidence planes; UElement operates the application and platform planes. A split-responsibility model for teams without platform-engineering capacity.

  • Sensitive planes never leave customer control
  • UElement carries patching, upgrades, and platform SLAs
  • Clear responsibility boundary written into the contract

Mid-market · Fintech

Managed SaaS

Fully hosted in a UElement environment. Zero local infrastructure and the fastest path to first signal for teams without a data-residency mandate.

  • Live in days rather than weeks
  • Elastic capacity with no procurement cycle
  • Upgrade path to sovereign or hybrid without re-platforming

How engagements run

Five gates, each with an outcome you can refuse to sign.

Implementation is fixed-price per gate. Nothing proceeds until the previous gate has produced something you can inspect.

G0Discovery & Scoping

A signed scoping pack: asset counts, zone barriers, integration inventory, and the agreed deployment shape.

2 weeks

G1Reference Deployment

Live telemetry from the candidate reference site, with any custom protocol parsers or connectors validated against real traffic.

4 weeks

G2Expansion & Enrichment

End-to-end identity stitching and data correlation across levels, sites, or business units.

6 weeks

G3Assurance & Tuning

Detection and alerting baselines tuned to the environment, operators trained, and final commercial sign-off.

4 weeks

G4Operate & Evolve

Governance reviews, threat-library updates, detection refreshes, and roadmap alignment.

Continuous

Licensing

Platform subscription is a modular annual licence sized by active assets and zones under management. Implementation is fixed-price per gate. Managed detection — where UElement operates the threat library directly — is an optional annual subscription.

Standard entry

Most engagements begin with a 45-day proof of value scoped to a single critical journey, site, or estate: a two-hour scoping workshop, a fourteen-day deployment, and a day-45 outcome review against agreed benchmarks.
